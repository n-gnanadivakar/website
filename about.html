About

Gnana Divakar.

Pronounced as, Gnaa-na Di-va-kar

Driving end-to-end product design for Zoho's enterprise SaaS suite. Leading UX strategy, crafting scalable design systems, and collaborating cross-functionally with engineering and product teams to ship user-centered solutions across web and mobile platforms.

My career began in motion and visual craft — as a Roto Artist at PhantomFX, then a Freelance Graphic Designer — before I moved into product. Each stop sharpened a different muscle: precision, storytelling, systems.

At BrokenGlass Designs, I learned to design with conviction and ship with speed. At Galaxy Weblinks, I scaled that craft across enterprise UX — flows, research, systems thinking. Today, at Zoho, I work on design that has to survive complexity.

Design, for me, is my IKIGAI — the meeting point of what I love, what I'm good at, what the world needs, and what I can build a life around.
