About

Gnana Divakar.

Pronounced as, Gnaa-na Di-va-kar

I own end-to-end product design for Zoho's enterprise SaaS suite — setting UX strategy, building the design systems and standards teams build from, and partnering closely with engineering and product to ship coherent experiences across web and mobile. My focus is the hard, structural work: making complex, multi-role systems feel simple.

My career began in motion and visual craft — as a Roto Artist at PhantomFX, then a Freelance Graphic Designer — before I moved into product. Each stop sharpened a different muscle: precision, storytelling, systems.

At BrokenGlass Designs, I learned to design with conviction and ship with speed. At Galaxy UX Studio, I scaled that craft across enterprise UX — flows, research, systems thinking. Today, at Zoho, I lead design that has to survive real enterprise complexity — and still feel effortless.

Design, for me, is my IKIGAI — the meeting point of what I love, what I'm good at, what the world needs, and what I can build a life around.
